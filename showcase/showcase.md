# Refract

A quiet bench. A handful of glowing nodes. One beautiful connection waiting to be found. Refract is a puzzle about drawing light, one deliberate turn at a time.

Press an outlined emitter and trace a beam through neighboring nodes. Join its matching emitter, thread every lens of the same shape, and leave no crystal charge unspent. Three colors share the bench, but their paths can never cross. Crystals bring those paths together—and make every choice matter.

There is room to experiment. Release a beam to leave it in place, pick it up again, or trace backward to rethink a turn. No timer counts down and no move is wasted. The light simply waits for you.

Follow the Campaign through 24 handcrafted studies, from a single strand of mint light to intricate three-channel constellations. Or settle into Cascade: an endless succession of fresh puzzles that grows more demanding every five boards. Find your rhythm. Draw the light.
